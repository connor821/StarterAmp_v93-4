# Fender Vibro-Champ (0082002000) - service diagram read

Source: `26189d1e-image.webp` (3656x2700, crisp CAD drawing, fully legible). Title block: "SERVICE DIAGRAM, COMBINED (schematic) VIBRO-CHAMP AMPLIFIER", Fender Musical Instruments, Corona CA. **Drawing number 0082002000, size D, Rev B, release date 26-OCT-10, sheet 1 of 2.** Revision table: Rev B, "EC4890", 27-APR-11, approved LEL. Drawn/engineered: Lorenzen. Database file Z1171.SCH. Notes: schematic is for PCB fabrication P/N 0082000000 and PCB assembly P/N 0082001000; resistors 5%, 1/2 W unless noted; AC voltages +/-10 % (volume at 50 %, input 20 mV, 1 kHz); **DC voltages measured with no signal**; polarized caps +/-20 %, 50 V min; unpolarized 10 %.

## 1. Tube complement
V1 12AX7A (V1-A, V1-B, preamp, plus heater V1-C); V2 6V6GT (V2-A power, heater V2-B); V3 5Y3GT (rectifier socket, with solid-state diodes D1/D2); V4 12AX7A (tremolo oscillator, V4-A/V4-B, heater V4-C). Single-ended output.

## 2. Preamp
- Inputs: J1 (input 1), J2 (input 2): J1 -> **R2 68k**, J2 -> **R3 68k**, both to a common node (X5) -> V1-A grid (pin 2) direct (no coupling cap). **R1 1M** from the J1 line to ground. Jack springs tie J2 to J1 line as in the earlier Champs (normalling, (?)).
- **V1-A:** plate (pin 1, node X7) load **R5 100k** to B+ node X8 (+265 V node). Cathode **R4 1.5k || C1 25 uF/50 V** to ground. Plate -> **C2 .022 uF/400 V** -> volume pot.
- **Volume R6 1M10A** (1 MEG, "10A" suffix, audio ("A") taper (?) - the suffix is as printed; no detail): top = C2, bottom = ground, wiper (CW arrow) -> V1-B grid (pin 7).
- **V1-B:** plate (pin 6, node X9) load **R8 100k** to X8. Cathode: **R7 1.5k || C10 25 uF/50 V** from pin 8 to a junction (X47), then **R17 47 ohm 1/2 W** to ground (X12). **NFB** and **tremolo injection** enter at this cathode network (see 4, 6). Plate -> **C3 .022 uF/400 V** -> V2-A grid.

## 3. Tone
None (volume only).

## 4. Tremolo (bias modulation of V1-B cathode)
- V4-A (12AX7A), V4-B (12AX7A). V4-A: grid (pin 2) through **R29 10k 1/2 W** to node X58; plate (pin 1, X30) load **R24 470k 1/2 W** to the 305 V node (X17) ; cathode (pin 3, X18) **R23 4.7k || C11 25 uF/50 V** to ground. **TP15 = +1.5 VDC** at V4-A cathode.
- Phase-shift network around V4-A: **C12 .033 uF/400 V** (from V4-A plate X30 up to line X39/X55), **C13 .01 uF/400 V** (X57 to X39), **C14 .01 uF/400 V** (X57 to X58), **R19 1M 1/2 W** (X58 to ground via X49), **R18 1M 1/2 W** (X57/J4 tip line to V4-A cathode X18/X50 via X50), plus **SPEED pot R26 3M10C (3 MEG, "C" taper (?))** with **R20 100k 1/2 W** in series (wiper strapped to the pot's end = rheostat) to ground. **S2** = pot-switch (contacts 1,2) near C12/X39 (function: on/off, exact switching topology (?) not resolvable). **J4 TREMOLO** jack (foot-switch) tip on X57; shown shorted to ground when unplugged (sleeve ground).
- V4-B: grid (pin 7) connected to the V4-A plate node; plate (pin 6) direct to the 305 V node (X17); cathode (pin 8, X52) **TP16 = 150 VDC** -> **R25 68k 1/2 W** -> node X54 -> **R21 22k 1/2 W** -> ground (X16). **R22 3.3k 1/2 W** from X54 (X53) to V1-B cathode (X14).
- Inconsistency (?): R22 ties X54 (DC ~37 V by simple divider 150 x 22/90) to V1-B's cathode, yet TP6 shows only +1.32 VDC at that cathode; either TP16/the node assignment differs from the drawing or the DC value is not what the divider suggests. Flag for the modeller; take the printed TP values at face value.

## 5. Phase inverter
None - single-ended.

## 6. Output stage
- **V2-A 6V6GT:** grid (pin 5) via **C3 .022/400 V** coupling; **R9 220k** grid leak to ground (X43); no grid stopper. Cathode (pin 8) **R10 Fr470 (470 ohm flameproof) 2 W || C4 25 uF/50 V** to ground. **Screen (pin 4) through R11 Fr470 2 W** (R11 sits on the V2 socket) to the 305 V node (wire "ORN", V2-1 pin NC). Plate (pin 3) to T2 BLU. T2 centre tap (RED) at the 350 V node (X19).
- **T2** output transformer P/N **0022905000** (primary BLU / RED; secondary YEL / BLK; impedance not printed). Secondary YEL -> node X11 -> speaker network -> J3 SPEAKER **4 OHMS**; BLK to ground.
- **Global NFB:** **R12 2.2k** from the secondary hot (X11, wire GRY, via X56) to the V1-B cathode network junction (X47, between R7 and R17): divider 2.2k : 47 ohm (derived fraction 47/(2200+47) ~ 2.1%; R7/C10 are above the injection node).
- **Speaker level switch S3 (HIGH / MIDDLE / LOW):** **R27 4 ohm 25 W** in series with the speaker hot line, **R28 8 ohm 25 W** shunt to ground on LOW. As drawn: HIGH shorts R27 (arm to the HIGH contact); MIDDLE leaves R27 in series; LOW connects the hot line to R28 (shunt). Exact contact logic (?) read from the symbol only.
- Printed voltages: **TP7 V2 plate +340 VDC / 185 VAC**, **TP8 V2 cathode +18.2 VDC**, **TP9 3.5 VAC** at the secondary (test condition: unit at rated line voltage, S2 [sic] at HIGH, 4 ohm load at J3, 20 mV rms 1 kHz in, vol up until min 5 W @ <10 % THD).

## 7. Power supply
- **T1** power transformer, 120 V version **P/N 0078046000** (100 V: 0078047000; 230/240 V: 0078048000). Line: plug (BLK/WHT/GRN), **S1 power switch**, **F1 1.25 A L 250 V 3AG (120 V and 100 V) / F1 1 A L 250 V 5x20 (230/240 V)** in the black line, **RT1 C60-11** NTC inrush thermistor in the white line, **C8 .1 uF/250 V** across the primary, primary has an internal BLK thermal fuse symbol.
- Secondaries: YEL pair (5 V rectifier filament) via **F2 T4A L 250 V**; RED / RED/YEL / RED HV winding: centre tap (RED/YEL) through **F4 T250mA L 250 V** to ground return; two **BYD33V** diodes **D1, D2** (mounted "on V3 socket"; V3 pins 3 and 7 NC) feed the 5Y3GT plate pins 4 and 6 (as drawn the tube is in series with the diodes); B+ from the rectifier cathode. GRN pair 6.3 V heater winding via **F3 T4A L 250 V**; artificial centre **R15 100 ohm / R16 100 ohm**; heater V1-C, V2-B, V4-C, panel lamp T47. **TP13/TP14 = 3.3 VAC** (each side to centre).
- Filter chain: **C7 22 uF/450 V** at X19 (**350 VDC TP12**, OT CT) -> **R14 "Fp10k" 10k 1 W (flameproof)** -> X17 **C6 10 uF/400 V** (**305 VDC TP11**: V2 screen via R11, V4 plates) -> **R13 22k 1 W** -> X15 **C5 10 uF/400 V** (**265 VDC TP10**, node X8: R5/R8 100k plate loads). Both C5/C6 grounds go to the chassis ground (BLK).
- B+ summary: 350 / 305 / 265 V.
