# Laney (Sabbath) and HiWatt (Pink) captures against Ironside and Hi-Wat Headroom (first-pass screen, b86 session)

**What this is.** 25 plug-in bounces of the test pack (Laney: Normal and Clipped presets; HiWatt: Normal, Brilliant, Jumped; each at All noon, Gain 2, Gain 10, "Bright" = Bass 0 / Mids 10 / Treble 10, "Scooped" = Bass 10 / Mids 0 / Treble 10, all else noon). Cab assumed OFF (not confirmed). The files could not be copied off the Mac (the file-transfer step refused every file), so each capture was measured on the Mac (levels and 1/3-octave spectra of the A pink-noise segments and the B plucked notes and chords), and our Hi-Wat Headroom and Ironside (4 voicings each, Gain 0.1 to 0.9) were rendered the same way here. **This is a coarser screen than the fitting tool: no harmonic (H2/H3) comparison.** Measured, not listened to.

**Gain structure (dB out per dB in, pink noise -30 to -18 dB; 1.0 = no squashing, 0 = fully squashed)**
- HiWatt (all three presets): Gain 2 = 0.54 to 0.80, noon = 0.11 to 0.27, Gain 10 = 0.01 to 0.03.
- Our Hi-Wat Headroom: Gain 0.1 = 0.92 to 0.95, 0.5 = 0.64 to 0.86, 0.9 = 0.07 to 0.47. It reaches the HiWatt's Gain 2 at about 0.5 but not its noon or Gain 10 (except voicing 3 at 0.9).
- Our Ironside: 0.1 = 0.54 to 0.79, 0.5 = 0.13 to 0.39, 0.9 = 0.00 to 0.04. Closer to the HiWatt noon and Gain 10, which is why it ranks first on most HiWatt captures.
- Laney (both presets): 0.00 to 0.12 at every Gain setting, including Gain 2. Always fully squashed. Ironside only gets there at Gain 0.9.

**Best matches (lower = closer; every score is loose, 2.2 to 4.3 dB, the full tool's tolerance is tighter)**
- HiWatt: Ironside Treble (v1) most often; Hi-Wat Headroom Brilliant-style (v2) only for the Scooped captures. Gain mapping on Ironside: Gain 2 -> 0.1 to 0.2, noon -> 0.5 to 0.6, Gain 10 -> 0.8 to 0.9.
- Laney Clipped: Ironside Fifty (v4) at 0.9 (noon, Bright), Linked (v3) at 0.8 to 0.9 for Gain 10 and Scooped. Laney Normal: Hi-Wat Headroom Linked (v3) 0.8 to 0.9 or Ironside Bass (v2) 0.8 to 0.9. All at the top of the Gain range.

**Tone differences (capture minus plug-in, after matching level)**
- HiWatt vs Hi-Wat Headroom / Ironside: 5 to 9 dB less at 63 to 80 Hz on the open E, about 4 dB less at 100 Hz, 1 to 3 dB less at 2 to 3 kHz, 5 to 12 dB more at 8 to 10 kHz (cab-off assumption matters here).
- Laney vs Ironside: 8 to 13 dB MORE at 80 to 125 Hz, 3 to 6 dB less at 1.6 to 3 kHz (Clipped: about 3 to 4 dB more above 4 kHz).
- Open-note decay (checked on a few): within about 1 dB per second of ours.
