## Dumble-style schematics (read, not built)

Eight sheets received: '60s preamp, power and output; '70s preamp, overdrive, power and output; and a 1997 full schematic (it marks confirmed values in red and probable values in blue). The sheets are image-only; I read the values off them by eye, so treat single digits as read, not verified. Voltages are as printed.

**'60s (Dumble-style) preamp.** Two 12AX7 triodes. Input: two jacks each through 68k to the V1 grid, 1M to ground; V1 cathode 1.5k with 25 uF; plate 100k (264 V). Coupling 500 pF to the treble pot (500k) top, and 98k / 0.1 uF to the bass pot (500k) with 0.01 uF and 9.6k to ground. Guitar/Mic switch swaps a 0.005 uF cap and 89.5k in the treble path. Deep switch adds 290k / 0.01 uF / 9.5k in the feedback leg. A 196k + 284k pair joins the tone network to the volume pot (1M), which has a 680 pF bright cap to its top. V2 (12AX7) cathode 1.5k / 25 uF, plate 100k (256 V), then a master volume (1M) into 97k, and 0.02 uF to the preamp out. No separate mid control.
**'60s power / output.** Four 6L6GC in two push-pull pairs, 470 ohm / 1 W screen resistors, 1500 ohm grid stoppers, plate about 435 V, 12AT7 long-tail phase inverter (plate about 291 V and 276 V, 47k and 51k loads, 0.05 uF couplings, 100k bias-feed resistors). Cathode of the inverter pair uses 22k and 458 ohm with 1M resistors to the input couplings (.001 uF and 0.1 uF); an Accent switch bypasses with 0.1 uF / 50 V. Global feedback 93 ohm from the speaker tap. Supply: two diodes, 80 uF / 450 V pair with 220k balancing resistors (437 V), choke, 2.9k and 11k dropping stages (436 / 412 / 384 V), bias from a 3.3k, diode and 100 uF / 150 V stage with a 10k pot and 27k.
**'70s preamp.** Two stages. V1: 22k grid stopper, 1M, cathode 1.5k / 5 uF, plate 100k. Tone stack: treble 250k with 330 pF; bass 250k with 1.8k to a 100k linear Middle pot; 0.1 uF Rock / 0.005 uF Jazz switch with 100k, 0.05 uF and 0.01 uF; a Deep switch with 270k + 270k, 1M, 0.01 uF and 10k. 500 pF bright cap across the volume pot (1M). V2: 220k grid stopper, 22M bias-feed, cathode 1.5k / 5 uF, plate 100k. Normal / Overdrive switch on the output.
**'70s overdrive.** Two more 12AX7 stages after the main preamp: trim 100k (linear) into 68k, 1.5k / 5 uF cathode, 100k plate, 500 pF across the plate load, 0.02 uF coupling and 100k into the Overdrive pot (100k linear); then 180k grid stopper, second stage identical, 0.01 uF coupling and a trim pot 100k.
**'70s power.** Six-diode full-wave supply, 100 uF / 350 V pair with 220k balancing resistors; standby; 80 ohm choke; rails A 460 V, B 454 V, C 436 V (2.2k / 1 W), D 306 V (22k / 2 W), E 303 V (1k / 1 W) with 20 uF filter caps; a separate 12 V relay supply for the footswitch.
**'70s output.** Four-tube push-pull stage with 3.3k grid stoppers, 500 ohm / 2 W screen resistors, 0.1 uF / 600 V couplings, 200k bias dividers, 100k balance pot; 12-series inverter at 308 V / 303 V with 1M, 24k and 820 ohm / 2 W in the tail; 390 ohm; 3.9k / 1 W feedback from the speaker tap; Accent 2.2 uF / 35 V.
**1997 full schematic (rev 1.2).** Channel 1 (clean): V1B stage with 22k stopper, 3.3k / 5 uF cathode, 220k plate, tone stack with 330 pF treble (250k), 0.1 uF, 0.01 uF, bass 500k, middle 250k, 9.8k, volume 1M with 220k / 390 pF; V1A second stage (150k plate, 3k3 / 5 uF cathode); clean master (1M) and an OD trim (100k) feeding the overdrive channel. Overdrive channel: V2B stage (68k stopper, 2k7 / 5 uF, 180k plate) into 0.0022 uF and a 250k Drive pot, then V2A (180k stopper, 1k8 / 1 uF, 120k plate) into a second stack with 330 pF, 0.022 uF caps, bass 1M, middle 20k, treble 250k and an OD level pot 1M. Power amp: 12AX7 long-tail inverter (0.01 uF, 0.1 uF, 24k, 820 ohm, 1M), 5k1 grid stoppers, 1k / 5 W screen resistors, two EL34, 4k7 feedback, presence 2k with 390 ohm and 1 uF. Supply rails 465 / 458 / 433 / 361 / 323 V.

Applied so far (v0.2), on Bumble Lead in Circuit-derived mode: the first two stages' cathode values (1.5k / 5 uF), the 500 pF bright cap, the overdrive channel's 0.02 uF coupling (one stage stands for the two), the '70s supply (436 V, 80 ohm choke, 2.2k with 20 uF), and the '70s Rock tone stack as a menu option (Tone Stack: Dumble-style). Not applied: the Jazz setting's shunt, the Deep switch, the second overdrive stage, the 6L6 and EL34 output stages' own curves (the existing low-feedback power family is used), and the '60s and '97 sheets as separate voicings. The '60s sheet is a four-6L6 amp with a plain Treble/Bass pair, the '70s adds a Middle pot and a two-stage overdrive channel, and the '97 sheet is a two-channel EL34 amp, so they would make three separate voicings if built.

## Friedman BE-100 (v2 rev 1.5), transcribed in b22
Preamp: four 12AX7 stages plus a switched fifth, relays for the CLN / BE / HBE selection (12 V from a 7812).
- V1A: grid 33k stopper, plate 220k + 100k with 500 pF across the 100k, cathode 2.7k / 0.68 uF. Coupling 2.2 nF into 1M + 68k.
- V2A: same stage again (220k + 100k with 500 pF, 2.7k / 0.68 uF); 33k grid stopper, 10M leak. Output through 2.2 nF into a switch network: a 22 nF "Fat" cap or 680 pF, then 68k (BE) or 560k, with 220k / 68k to ground selected by the channel switch. **The 2.2 nF into 68k puts the low-cut corner near 1 kHz in the tightest setting**: this is the BE-100's tightness, and it is a coupling network, not a tone control.
- V2B: plate 100k, cathode 2.7k / 0.68 uF; 2.2 nF into the 1M Gain pot, then 470k into V3A, with 500 pF across the 470k (bright) and 470k to ground.

- Saturation switch: 10k + 100 nF into a pair of MPSA06 transistors (base-emitter junctions as the clipper) with a 1M, off the cathode follower.
- Tone stack: 33k slope resistor, 500 pF treble cap on a 250k pot, 22 nF bass cap on a 1M pot, 22 nF mid cap on a 25k pot, then 220k into the 1M Master. This is the JCM800 stack (same values to within 470 pF vs 500 pF).
- Clean channel: V1B (plate 100k, cathode 820 / 10 uF), bright cap 100 pF or 220 pF across the Clean Vol pot, output 22 nF into 470k / 470k.
- Effects loop: two LND150 depletion MOSFETs (send buffer and return), with a 50k send trim and a 1M return level.

### JC-120 channel 2 preamp, read from the 1979 scan (b25; hand-read, so any single value may be misread)
- **Signal path, in order:** High (19 mV) or Low (59 mV, 7.8 to 10 dB less sensitive) input, 33k / 68k series resistors, 0.082 uF coupling, a JFET (Q1, 1M gate resistor), then a **passive Treble / Bass / Middle stack** (Treble 250kB with 150 pF and 0.082 uF, Bass 250kA with 470k, Middle 10kB with 1k and 0.047 uF, 12k and 100k source resistors), then a second JFET (Q2) that recovers the stack's loss (annotated 180 mV in, 24 mV after the stack, 21 mV at Q2's gate).
- **Distortion stage sits after the tone stack:** a JFET stage (Q3) with a pair of 1S2473 diodes (D1, D2) back to back to ground through 33k and 15k; the Distortion pot (10kC with a push switch) and a 330 ohm / 1/50 uF network set the stage's gain. So the clipping is **diode clamping on a small signal, after the EQ**: bass and treble settings change how hard it clips, and the Middle control barely moves the tone (matches the spec sheet's 0.6 to 0.8 dB).
- **Then:** two more JFET stages (Q4 with D3, and Q5, 20 mV at its gate), the Volume pot (1MB) with a Bright switch (220 pF across the pot), and a 12k stage into the power amp (about 200 mV at the output of the stage chain; the power amp is the transistor stage already noted above).
- **Reverb:** NJM4558 (IC1) driver and recovery around the spring unit, Reverb pot 10kB, mixed back through a JFET (Q19). The Distortion foot switch grounds the distortion pot's switch line. The chorus is the MN3007 / MN3101 circuit on the same sheet.
- **Rails:** +30 V for the JFET stages, +15 V for the op-amps and chorus, +24 V and +12.5 V bias taps.
- **What this means for the Jade Chorus model (not changed yet):** clean JC voicing = JFET front end, stack first, very flat Middle, volume after the stack, no output transformer (hard rail clipping). Its Distortion voicing should be a diode clamp after the EQ, not a tube-style stage. Needs a real capture or measurement to set the numbers; nothing here was fitted or listened to.


## Twin Reverb (1972) and AC30 (1978) re-read for the reverb and the amps (b33, analysis only)
Both sheets are readable at full size (the Twin is a clean redraw; the AC30 is a hand-drawn 1978 sheet, so single values may be misread). Nothing here was built or listened to.

**Twin Reverb, reverb section.**
- **Driver:** 12AT7, both halves in parallel, one 2.2k / 25 uF cathode, plate loaded by the reverb transformer (part 022921) whose secondary goes to the tank's input jack. Low gain, transformer-coupled, a fairly clean drive that clips softly only when hit hard. The tube is fed from the vibrato channel's output through 500 pF (so the driver gets a bass-cut signal) into a 1M grid resistor.
- **Recovery:** the tank's pickup goes to a jack with **2 nF and 220k across it**, then a 12AX7 (V4A) with 820 ohm / 25 uF cathode and 100k plate load, then **0.003 uF into the 100k Reverb pot**. That coupling is a high-pass at about **530 Hz** (6 dB per octave), so the returned reverb is thin and bright: this is the classic Fender "drip". The pot wiper goes through 470k into the mixer stage (V4B) with the dry signal.
- **Pickup resonance:** the pickup coil with the 2 nF across it forms a resonance. The coil inductance is not on the sheet; for 0.5 to 1.5 H (my assumption) it falls at about **3 to 5 kHz**, which is the spring "sizzle".
- **Tank itself:** not on the sheet (only "reverb input" and "reverb output" jacks).

**Twin Reverb, power amp (re-read, corrects what I wrote earlier).** Four 6L6GC with 1.5k grid stoppers and 470 ohm / 1 W **screen** resistors to the B rail; cathodes go straight to ground (fixed bias from a diode, 470 or 1k, 50 uF / 70 V, 3.3k, 15k and a 10k balance pot; the bias voltage is not printed). 12AT7 phase inverter (330k, 33k, 470 ohm, 47k loads, 68k to the grids). Global feedback: **820 ohm** from the 4 ohm tap into a node with 100 ohm to ground, a fraction of about 0.11 (**about -19 dB**). Supply: three diodes per leg, 2 x 70 uF / 350 V with 220k balancing, choke, then 20 uF stages: 2.2k to the second (0.044 s) and 10k to the preamp rail (0.2 s). No voltages are printed.

**AC30 (1978), reverb section.**
- **Driver:** ECC83, both halves in parallel, 10 nF in from the mixed channel bus, 1M grid resistor, **1k / 100 uF cathode** (about 3.2 V), plates into transformer **T8110**, then to the pan labelled **Accutronics 4AB2A1B**. Another drive-stage with a transformer; no tube clipping information beyond the bias point.
- **Recovery:** the tank output goes through 22k into V12A (ECC83, 220k to ground, 100k plate load, 2k2 cathode with a small capacitor (4.7 nF or 4.7 uF, I cannot tell which), then **1 nF into the 100k log Reverb Volume pot** (high-pass at about **1.6 kHz**, brighter than the Twin), 470k to a second stage (V12B, 4k7 cathode) and 100 nF into the phase inverter input. A footswitch shorts the reverb signal off.
- **Tank code (corrected after checking Amplified Parts' Accutronics page, b33):** "4AB2A1B" is a seven-character code: type 4 (the long tank, about 16.25 in), input impedance letter A = **8 ohm**, output impedance letter B = **2,250 ohm**, decay digit **2 = medium (1.75 to 3.0 s)**, then connector, locking and mounting codes. (My first reading, that the 2 was the spring count, was wrong.) Type 4 is described as "four counter-wound and coupled springs"; sellers list the 4AB3C1x tanks as "2-spring", so the count depends on how pairs are counted. The tank DC resistance and inductance are not published there. The Twin's tank is not named on its sheet; the common long-decay Type 4 (4AB3C1x: 8 ohm in, 2,250 ohm out, 2.75 to 4.0 s) is the usual Fender part, but that is not from the sheet.

**AC30 (1978), the rest.**
- **Top Boost stack confirmed:** Bright channel V4A, then V6A / V6B cathode follower (56k load), 50 pF, 22 nF, 22 nF, 10k, 1M log treble, 1M log bass, 470k to the mixer. These are exactly the standard values already used.
- **Cut control (not modelled):** a 250k log pot in series with 4.7 nF between the two phase-inverter outputs (across the power-tube grids), fed from about 50 to 70k. With my assumed source resistance the response at the extremes is: pot at 250k, about -3 to -4 dB at all frequencies above 500 Hz (nearly no cut); pot at 0, a first-order roll-off starting near 250 to 700 Hz (about -10 dB at 1 kHz, -20 dB at 4 kHz).
- **Power amp:** four EL84, 100R and 1k5 per tube (the 100R goes to the B+ line, so it is the screen resistor), 220k grid leaks, **one shared 47 ohm / 250 uF cathode (about 11 V)**, plates about 370 V, preamp rails 310 / 290 / 245 V, bridge of four 1N4007 into a 64 uF / 450 V reservoir, 22k and 6k8 droppers with 32 uF. No global feedback. The 22k x 32 uF = 0.7 s already used for the slow droop matches.
- **Other:** a 12AX7 long-tail inverter (ECC83), vibrato / tremolo channel with a three-stage phase-shift oscillator, "L.S. Celestion G12 1871" speaker note.

**What this gives the spring reverb.** The circuits give the *electronics around* the tank (drive transformer, recovery filter, pickup loading), not the tank. Numbers usable now: a return high-pass of 530 Hz (Twin) or 1.6 kHz (AC30), a pickup resonance (2 nF, about 3 to 5 kHz), transformer-coupled drive with a fairly clean headroom, and for the AC30 two recovery gain stages. The current Spring Drive (high-pass 40 to 140 Hz, low-pass 12 to 5.5 kHz, soft ceiling) is generic and sits much lower than either amp.

**What this gives the amps.** Twin Sparkle: nothing new that would change the sound much (stack, stage curves, supply and feedback family are in already); only the fixed-bias note corrects the record. Chime Thirty: the Cut control is the one real gap, and the bias, supply and no-feedback power stage are already in the family used. Neither sheet gives anything the amps need captures for (breakup feel, speaker, tube curves).
