Starter Amp v0.1 beta (Windows, 64-bit)

Standalone: double-click "Starter Amp v0.1.exe". Choose your audio interface in the Audio settings
(an ASIO driver gives the lowest delay; the Windows default driver works but is slower).

Plug-in: copy the folder "Starter Amp v0.1.vst3" into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.

Windows may show a blue "protected your PC" screen because this beta is not code-signed.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

To report a problem: say what you did, your Windows version, your audio interface and driver,
and the version label shown in the corner of the window (v0.1).
