# Fairchild 660/670 - condensed notes

Source: https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/fairchild-660-670 (Sound On Sound review). Filed as a condensed summary read through the web fetch tool, not a copy of the page. No schematic or manual yet: send one if you want the sidechain modelled.

- Origin: early 1950s, Rein Narma, Fairchild. 660 = mono, 670 = dual channel (L-R or M-S switch). Used for level control and cutting. Abbey Road standard from the mid 1960s.
- Hardware: 20 valves, 11 transformers; most of the parts are in the sidechain. Audio path: input transformer, one push-pull variable-mu stage (four 6386 dual triodes per channel, four triode elements in parallel per half), output transformer. 30 min warm-up recommended. 6386 is out of production.
- Sidechain tapped after the gain cell, so it is a feedback design, programme dependent.
- Controls: input in 1 dB steps, threshold, six-position time constant switch, meter GR / plate current, internal trim for knee hardness.
- Time constants: pos 1 attack 0.2 ms, release 0.3 s; pos 2 0.2 ms, 0.8 s; pos 3 0.4 ms, 2 s; pos 4 0.8 ms, 5 s; pos 5 0.4 ms, 2 s (single peaks) / 10 s (multiple peaks); pos 6 0.2 ms, 0.3 s / 10 s / 25 s (programme adaptive).
- Ratio and knee: roughly 1:1 to 2:1 for small peaks, rising to about 20:1 on loud signals; soft knee, trim pot hardens it.
- Character quoted: fast attack with long release, little audible pumping; fastest release 300 ms is too slow for 1176-style pumping.
