# dbx 160A cut sheet and the Sound on Sound dbx 160 review (read Oct 2026)

Sources: the dbx 160A cut sheet (zzounds-hosted PDF, fetched as text; no local PDF copy was possible through the web tool) and the Sound on Sound review "Waves dbx 160" (describes the original).

160A cut sheet figures:
- Detector: true RMS. A separate detector input (1/4 in TRS, above 200 kohm, +28 dBu max) lets an external (for example equalised) signal drive the compression.
- Attack (program dependent): 15 ms for a 10 dB step, 5 ms for 20 dB, 3 ms for 30 dB.
- Release (program dependent): 8 ms for 1 dB, 80 ms for 10 dB, 400 ms for 50 dB; stated rate 125 dB per second.
- Knee: switchable OverEasy (soft) or hard knee on the 160A. The original 160 was hard knee only (SoS).
- Ratio: variable 1:1 through infinity:1 through negative compression to -1:1. More than 60 dB maximum compression.
- Threshold: -40 to +20 dBu.
- Topology: the sheet does not say; SoS calls the original feed-forward with RMS side-chain and a discrete VCA.

Used for: the RMS (VCA) compressor style (b59). Not used: negative ratios, the external detector input, OverEasy.
