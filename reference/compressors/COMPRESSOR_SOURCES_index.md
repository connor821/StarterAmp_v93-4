# Compressor sources filed (b58)

All in `sources/` unless noted. Numbers below are taken from the sheets as read; nothing has been modelled from them yet.

| File | What it is | Useful numbers / content |
|---|---|---|
| `Fairchild_660-670info.pdf` | Fairchild technical bulletin and specification sheet (scanned, 2 pages) | Attack 0.2 ms (positions 1, 2, 6), 0.4 ms (3, 4, 5). Release from 10 dB of limiting: 0.3 s, 0.8 s, 2 s, 5 s; position 5 auto 2 s single peaks / 10 s multiple peaks; position 6 auto 0.3 s / 10 s / 25 s. Ratio is a function of the amount of limiting and the two threshold controls, 1:2 to 1:30. Gain 7 dB (16 dB for the 660); response 30 Hz to 15 kHz +/-1.5 dB; THD under 1 % at +18 dBm; tubes 8 x 6386, 1 x "6084" (as printed, hard to read), 1 x 5651, 2 x 12AX7, 2 x 12BH7, 1 x EL34, 4 x 6973, 1 x GZ34 (670). The bulletin names no sidechain details. |
| `LA-2A_manual.pdf` | Universal Audio LA-2A reissue manual, 21 pages | Optical (T4 electro-luminescent cell). Release about 0.06 s for 50 % release, 0.5 to 5 s for complete release, depending on how much reduction came before ("two-stage" release). Compress and Limit switch changes the ratio (gentle vs higher). Tubes 2 x 12AX7A, 12BH7A, 6AQ5. 600 ohm in and out. Theory and calibration sections. |
| `Distressor.pdf` | Empirical Labs Distressor user manual (Manualslib copy), 19 pages | Attack 50 us to 30 ms, release 0.05 s to 3.5 s (up to 20 s in the 10:1 "Opto" mode). Eight ratio curves from 1:1 to Nuke, plus an opto curve; 2:1 has a +15 dB soft knee. Distortion modes (0.02 % to 20 %). Detector high-pass and band emphasis in the sidechain. Dynamic range 110 dB. |
| `SSL_G-Series_Bus_Compressor_500_Module_User_Guide.pdf` | SSL G-series bus compressor, 500 series module, 8 pages | Operation text only: VCA always in circuit, sidechain on IN switch; attack, ratio and release are switches; the knee point moves with the ratio (a lower ratio lowers the effective threshold); dominant sidechain (L and R rectified separately by a true peak full wave detector, the louder channel controls both); newer revision has a sidechain high-pass switch. No values for the switch positions. |
| `SSL_Mixbus_Compressor_Clone.pdf` | DIY clone write-up of the SSL mix bus compressor (THAT 2180/2181 VCA, 11 pages) | Ratio 2:1, 4:1, 10:1. Attack 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30 ms. Release 0.1, 0.3, 0.6, 1.2 s and 2.4 s (auto). The auto release combines two time constants (91k + 6.8 uF and 750k + 0.47 uF), short for short peaks and longer for sustained compression. Sidechain: dummy VCA, two full-wave rectifier stages, offset and gain set by the ratio switch, the threshold sets sidechain VCA gain, effective threshold 0.6 V (diode drop). |
| `Boss_CS-3_service_notes_1986.pdf` | Boss CS-3 Compression Sustainer service notes (arrived with no name), 2 pages | Parts list and layout: VCA is a uPC1252H2, op-amps M5218L and BA718, FETs 2SK117 and 2SK30ATM. Controls: Level 50k, Tone 20k, Attack 250k, Sustain 20k. 9 V, 10 mA, input 1 Mohm, equivalent input noise -110 dBm. The schematic page is in the PDF (not yet read in detail). |
| `Pigtronix_Philosopher_Tone_Micro_trace_schematic.pdf` | Philosopher's Tone Micro trace schematic, 1 page | Already used for the compressor mode; includes the VTL5C6 LDR/LED pair, TL074, NE5532 and 1N914 diode network. |

## Keeley Compressor Plus Germanium (page, not a file)
https://robertkeeley.com/product/keeley-compressor-plus-germanium/ : controls Level, Compression, Tone, Blend. Keeley says it uses a "Japanese-style germanium transistor" (2SD352 named in the tags) in the output section and in the sidechain feedback. The page does not say what the gain element is (OTA, FET or optical) and gives no schematic, so there is nothing to model from the page alone. 9 to 18 V, 25 mA, true or buffered bypass, price $189.
